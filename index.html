Strategic insights

01
The discount battle is intensifying

Exoticca and TourRadar are fighting an aggressive price war — Exoticca claiming up to 70% off and creating artificial sale events ("Spring Black Friday") to manufacture urgency. Intrepid is running a more modest sale at 20% off (extended to 22 May), anchored to brand values. Most premium operators — Bunnik, Flashpack, Exodus, APT — are not discounting at all, signalling a clear market split.

02
Flights included is a key conversion lever

Exoticca, Trailfinders, and Tripadeal consistently lead with "flights included" as a headline hook. This creates a direct comparison challenge for land-only operators. If your products exclude flights, this is worth monitoring closely — it significantly shapes how customers perceive value.

03
Hot destinations right now

Spain & Portugal are being pushed hardest by Exoticca. South America (Peru, Patagonia, Iguazu) is getting strong 2027 forward-booking treatment from Bunnik. Asia (Vietnam, Japan, Cambodia) features prominently across Contiki and Intrepid. Bali & Fiji dominate Luxury Escapes. Africa is emerging as a 2027 opportunity with Bunnik launching early.

04
The market is bifurcating

The travel email market is clearly splitting into two camps: discount/urgency operators (Exoticca, TourRadar, Tripadeal, Trailfinders) heavy on price and FOMO, vs. lifestyle/values brands (Flashpack, WeRoad, Contiki, Bunnik, Exodus) focused on community, purpose, and premium experiences. Intrepid is attempting to straddle both — a useful case study to watch.

05
2027 forward booking is an early mover opportunity

Bunnik Tours is aggressively pushing 2027 itineraries right now for South America and Africa. This early forward-booking strategy, combined with premium Belmond partnerships, locks in high-value customers before competitors enter. Consider whether any 2027 destinations in your portfolio should be promoted now to capture early demand.

06
Content marketing as a brand differentiator

Contiki (six-two blog) and WeRoad are using editorial content — friendship, cultural immersion, travel tips — as a primary email strategy rather than promotions. Exodus uses traveller testimonials and post-booking upsell content. This approach builds engagement and brand affinity without eroding price perception — worth considering if your positioning is premium or values-led.
